# Iwate.AI Landing Page Context

register: brand

## Product Purpose

Iwate.AI is an Iwate-born, free AI learning community for people who are interested in generative AI but do not yet know where to start. The landing page lowers anxiety, explains what members can do in the LINE-based community, and moves visitors toward free participation.

## Audience

- General residents who want to try AI for daily research, planning, and organization.
- Working adults who want to use AI for documents, summaries, ideas, and business efficiency.
- Students who want to use AI for study, reports, job hunting, and future skills.
- People connected to Iwate who prefer a local, approachable learning environment over a flashy expert-only AI community.

## Brand Tone

Friendly, local, practical, encouraging, and accessible. The page should feel like a safe first step, not a technical seminar or venture-backed SaaS product.

## Strategic Principles

- The primary action is free LINE participation.
- Show that beginners are welcome before explaining advanced AI benefits.
- Keep the local Iwate identity visible through copy, mountain/region motifs, and the founder story.
- Use hand-drawn illustration energy, soft color, and plain Japanese copy to make AI feel approachable.
- Avoid intimidating jargon, enterprise SaaS aesthetics, dark cyber themes, and generic AI gradient templates.

## Success Criteria

Visitors should understand within the first viewport that Iwate.AI is free, beginner-friendly, based on LINE, and useful for general people, working adults, and students. The page should provide enough trust and detail to make a form or LINE opt-in feel low risk.
